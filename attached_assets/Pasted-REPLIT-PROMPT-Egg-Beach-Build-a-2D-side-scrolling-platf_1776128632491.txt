REPLIT PROMPT — "Egg Beach" 🥚🏖️

Build a 2D side-scrolling platformer game called "Egg Beach" using HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript (no external game libraries). The game must be fully playable on both desktop (WASD controls) and mobile iOS (touch controls). Here are the complete specifications:

🎨 Visual Style

Characters (Lola & Dawn): Pixel art sprites drawn programmatically on canvas.
Lola: A pixel art girl with blonde hair, blue eyes, orange skin, purple top, purple skirt, blue shoes, and a small orange flower on her head (see pixel art style reference).
Dawn: A pixel art black chicken with a red comb and small yellow beak.
Backgrounds: Flat illustrated style resembling hand-drawn marker art with horizontal color bands. Each level has its own background:

Level 1 (Beach): Light blue sky with white puffy clouds, a navy blue ocean band, yellow sandy ground with an orange/brown stripe.
Level 2 (Underwater): Deep blue background, sandy seafloor, coral and seaweed decorations in red/green.
Level 3 (Forest): Light blue sky, dense wall of tall brown tree trunks with thick green canopy across the top, yellow/green ground.


UI: Clean pixel font for all text. Score bar at top showing: eggs collected, timer countdown, lives remaining (as small egg icons).


🗺️ Game Structure
Start Screen

Title: "Egg Beach" in large bold marker-style font
Blue horizontal line background texture
"Tap to Start" or "Press Enter" prompt
Simple animated Lola sprite standing idle

Between Level Story Cards
Display a full-screen illustrated card with story text before each level:

Before Level 1: "Lola loves eggs. Dawn the chicken has the best eggs on the beach. But Dawn won't share — she runs! Chase her down before she reaches the volcano!"
Before Level 2: "Dawn fled into the sea! Lola dives in after her, dodging jellyfish through the waves..."
Before Level 3: "Dawn emerges in the forest, sprinting for the volcano! Lola must catch her NOW before it's too late!"
Win Screen: "Lola catches Dawn just in time! The eggs are safe." with large "YOU WIN!" text and a drawing of a small brown barn/house with green grass.


🎮 Controls

Desktop: WASD to move and jump (W = jump)
Mobile: On-screen touch buttons: Left arrow, Right arrow, Jump button (up arrow), displayed at bottom of screen
Double jump is supported — Lola can jump a second time while airborne


👧 Lola — Player Character

Moves left and right, jumps, and double jumps
Default speed: Slightly faster than Dawn's starting speed (tense from the start)
5 lives — shown as egg icons in the HUD. Losing all 5 = full game reset to Start Screen
Lola must collect a minimum of 5 eggs before she is "strong enough" to catch Dawn. If she touches Dawn with fewer than 5 eggs, nothing happens.
Catching Dawn (touching her with 5+ eggs collected) = level complete


🐔 Dawn — The Chicken

Runs ahead of Lola, frequently going off-screen
An arrow indicator always points toward Dawn's position when she is off-screen
Dawn's behavior each level:

Level 1: Moderate speed, occasional direction change
Level 2: Faster, jumps and dodges more
Level 3: Fastest, aggressive dodging and direction changes


Dawn randomly drops colored eggs on the ground as she runs (10 eggs per level)
Dawn can jump, dodge left/right, and reverse direction


🥚 Egg Power-Ups
Dawn drops 10 eggs per level, randomly placed on the ground as she runs. Each egg color grants a power-up to Lola that lasts 10 seconds:
ColorEffect🔴 RedSpeed boost🔵 BlueHigher jump🟢 GreenStronger (can catch Dawn from slightly farther)🟡 YellowMore agile (faster direction changes)🟣 PurpleDamage resistance (next obstacle hit doesn't cost a life)
Eggs are drawn as simple pixel oval shapes in their respective colors with a shine dot.

⏱️ Timer & Lose Conditions

60 second timer per level, displayed in the HUD
If timer runs out: Game Over — return to Start Screen
If Lola loses all 5 lives: Game Over — return to Start Screen
Lava at the end of Level 3 (right side of screen, visually a black volcano with red lava streams) — touching lava costs a life immediately


🏖️ Level 1 — The Beach

Background: Blue sky, white clouds, navy ocean band, yellow sand, orange ground stripe
Scrolling: Continuous left-to-right
Obstacle: Ocean waves that periodically surge up from the bottom of the screen. Drawn as dark blue curved wave shapes. Contact costs a life.
Platform: Flat sandy ground, mostly even with small bumps
Crabs walk along the ground as minor obstacles — contact costs a life. Drawn as simple red pixel crabs.


🌊 Level 2 — Underwater

Background: Deep blue, sandy floor, coral/seaweed decorations
Movement: Lola can both walk on the seafloor AND swim (floaty jump physics — slower fall speed)
Obstacle: Jellyfish float up and down in the water column. Drawn as simple purple/pink dome shapes with trailing tentacles. Contact costs a life.
No air meter — Lola can breathe underwater freely


🌲 Level 3 — The Forest

Background: Dense brown tree trunks, thick green canopy, yellow-green ground
Obstacles:

Tree roots on the ground to jump over
Low-hanging branches to duck under (add a crouch mechanic — S key / swipe down on mobile)
Volcano at the far right end of the level — drawn as a black mountain with red lava streams. Reaching the volcano without catching Dawn = Game Over.


Flowers scattered on the ground (red, green, blue pixel flowers matching the storyboard art)


📊 HUD (Heads Up Display)
Display at the top of the screen at all times:

Left: Row of 5 egg icons representing lives (filled = alive, empty = lost)
Center: Countdown timer (60 → 0) in large pixel font
Right: Eggs collected counter (e.g., "🥚 3/10") — turns green when 5+ collected


🏗️ Technical Requirements

Pure HTML5 Canvas + vanilla JavaScript, single index.html file
All sprites drawn programmatically (no external image assets required)
60fps game loop using requestAnimationFrame
Responsive canvas — scales to fill the screen on both mobile and desktop
Touch events for mobile: touchstart, touchmove, touchend
Collision detection for: eggs, obstacles, Dawn, lava, level boundaries
Game states: START, STORY, PLAYING, GAME_OVER, WIN
Parallax scrolling background (foreground elements scroll faster than background)
Dawn's off-screen arrow indicator updates in real time


🎵 Audio

Placeholder functions for sound effects (egg collect, jump, life lost, level complete) — leave as empty functions with comments so sounds can be added later
No background music for now


Build the complete game in a single index.html file. Make it fun, colorful, and charming — matching the energy of a hand-drawn game made by a kid and their parent. 🎨
